Anti-Spam Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

incro sends email only to people who have a genuine reason to hear from us — clients, prospects with a clear business relevance, and subscribers who asked to be kept informed. This page explains how we keep our outreach and marketing email consent-based, identifiable, and easy to opt out of, and how to report a message that falls short of it.

Our commitment

We do not send unsolicited bulk email, and we do not buy, rent, or scrape email lists for mass distribution. Every message we send — whether a newsletter, a product update, or a piece of outbound business development — is sent by a real person at incro, to a real recipient we have a documented reason to contact, and it always says who it's from.

What counts as spam, to us

We treat a message as spam if it does any of the following:

  • Is sent in bulk to recipients who never had a relationship with incro or never opted in
  • Hides or falsifies the sender's identity, reply-to address, or originating domain
  • Uses a subject line that misrepresents the content of the message
  • Omits a working, honored way to opt out
  • Continues after a recipient has unsubscribed or asked to stop
  • Harvests addresses from websites, forums, or other sources without consent

We hold our own outreach to this same standard, and we expect the same from anyone sending on our behalf.

How we build and use our lists

  • Business development outreach goes to contacts with a clear, documented business relevance to incro's services — for example, a finance or operations leader at a company matching our client profile. We rely on legitimate interest as our legal basis where applicable, and we honor opt-outs immediately.
  • Newsletter and content subscribers are added only after they sign up directly, and every send includes a one-click way to leave the list.
  • Client and partner communications are sent to people we have an existing business relationship with, about that relationship.

We do not sell, rent, or share our email lists with third parties for their own marketing use.

Opting out

Every marketing or outreach email from incro includes a clear way to stop hearing from us. You can also opt out at any time by:

  • Replying with "unsubscribe" or "remove me" to any message we send
  • Using the unsubscribe link included in the message
  • Emailing us directly at hello@incro.io

We process opt-out requests immediately and suppress the address from future sends. No further marketing or outreach email is sent from that list once a request is received.

Sender identification

Every email we send:

  • Identifies incro as the sender, with a real name and role behind the message
  • Uses a subject line that accurately reflects the content
  • Includes a valid reply-to address that is actively monitored
  • Is sent from infrastructure we authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC

List hygiene

We monitor bounce rates, spam complaints, and engagement on our sending domains, and we remove invalid, unengaged, or complaining addresses on a rolling basis. We do not send to role-based or generic addresses harvested without context, and we periodically clean our lists to keep deliverability and sender reputation healthy for everyone we send to.

Reporting a concern

If you believe you've received an email from incro that violates this policy — sent without a fair basis for contacting you, missing an opt-out, or otherwise inconsistent with what's described here — please forward it to us. Include the full message with headers if possible.

We review every report and take corrective action, including removing the recipient and, where relevant, adjusting our sending practices.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our practices or applicable regulations change. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
